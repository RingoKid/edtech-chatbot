# Handwriting & Spelling Lesson

## A. Standards

- **K.F.PA.6.a** — Isolate and pronounce initial, final, and then medial sounds in spoken words with three phonemes
- **K.F.P.1.a** — Identify and produce basic one-to-one phoneme-grapheme correspondences for the most frequently used sound for each consonant
- **K.F.P.3.a** — Use knowledge of letter/sound correspondences to connect letters (graphemes) to sounds (phonemes) to encode simple one-syllable words, including high-frequency words (VC, CV, CVC)
- **K.F.H.1.a** — Form all uppercase and lowercase letters

## B. Objectives

- Identify the /b/ sound in the beginning of CVC words and briefly review hearing sounds at the end of CVC words
- Compare the /b/ sound to /p/ and connect the sound /b/ to the letters **B** and **b**
- Trace and write uppercase **B** and lowercase **b** using correct letter formation
- Spell simple CVC words that begin with /b/ using known sound-letter correspondences

## C. Materials

- Sound cards or sticky notes for **m, a, c, p, d, t, l, g, n, j, b**
- Teacher sound card for **b**
- Individual student sound cards for Tap that Sound
- Whiteboard, chart paper, or pocket chart
- Handwriting model showing uppercase **B** and lowercase **b**
- Dotted-line handwriting sheets for uppercase **B** and lowercase **b**
- Pencils
- Handwriting paper with lines
- Elkonin boxes with 3 sound boxes
- Sound-Letter Hunt recording sheet with 3 boxes
- Letter cards for **m, a, c, p, d, t, l, g, n, j, b**
- Activities: **Tap that Sound**, **Sound Hunt**, **SkyWrite**, **Trace & Say**, **Sound-Letter Hunt**
