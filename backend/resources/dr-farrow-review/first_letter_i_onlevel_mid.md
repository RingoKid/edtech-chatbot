# Handwriting & Spelling Lesson

## A. Standards

- 1.F.PA.6.a — Isolate and pronounce initial, medial, and final sounds in spoken one-syllable words, including words with digraphs and blends
- 1.F.P.1.b — Identify and produce both long and short vowel sounds for A, E, I, O, U, including final -e and vowel digraphs
- 1.F.P.3.a — Encode regularly spelled one-syllable words with a variety of spelling patterns (VC, CVC, CVCe, VCC, CCVC, CVCC, CCVCC), including high-frequency words
- 1.F.H.1.a — Form all uppercase and lowercase letters and words with accuracy and consistency

## B. Objectives

- Identify the letter-sound correspondence for **i**
- Discriminate between short **/i/** in CVC words and long **/ī/** in CVCe words
- Trace and write uppercase **I** and lowercase **i** accurately
- Segment sounds in one-syllable words with medial **i**
- Spell CVC and CVCe words with accuracy using the vowel sound to choose the pattern

- Mini whiteboards and dry erase markers

🗣️ **Teacher:** "Now it is time for **Stretch-and-Write**. We are going to listen to words, stretch the sounds, and write the letters in the boxes. We have to think carefully about the middle vowel. If the vowel is short /i/, we will write a CVC word. If the vowel is long /ī/, we will write a CVCe word."

🗣️ **Teacher:** "Before you do it by yourself, let me show you. My turn. The word is **fin**. So, I first stretch the sounds, /f/ /i/ /n/. The beginning sound is /f/. I know that the /f/ sound is written with the letter **f**. I am going to write **f** in the first box. So, we have /f/ /i/. The /i/ sound is short /i/, so I write the letter **i** in the middle box. Now I hear /n/ at the end, so I write **n** in the last box. **Fin** is a CVC word because the vowel is short."

🗣️ **Teacher:** "Let me do one more. My turn. The word is **fine**. I stretch the sounds: /f/ /ī/ /n/. I hear /f/ first, so I write **f** in the first box. I hear /ī/ in the middle.
