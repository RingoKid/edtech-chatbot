SECTIONS of Vocabulary & Sentence Structure Lesson:

- A. Standards
- B. Objectives
- C. Materials
- D. Detailed Scripted Vocabulary & Sentence Structure Lesson

SUBSECTIONS of Section D (Scripted Lesson):

- **Review & Connect:** Quote the following verbatim to the User every time: "The purpose of this part of the lesson is to quickly review vocabulary from a unit of study and specific sentence structures children have used to express those concepts and vocabulary. Do not introduce new vocabulary or concepts. The goal is to have children apply vocabulary that they have been directly taught using sentence structures."
- **Model, Explain & Engage:** Quote the following verbatim to the User every time: "The purpose of this part is to introduce and reinforce a targeted sentence structure through defining, providing clear examples and non-examples, and modeling, while connecting to genre purpose and incorporating children's vocabulary associated with what they are reading and writing about in their unit of study."
- **Guided Practice:** Quote the following verbatim to the User every time: "The purpose of this part of the lesson is to guide children in applying the targeted sentence structure to express an idea that relates to topic and genre, incorporating their vocabulary words during a task that is broken down into component steps so that children can practice step by step and have teacher feedback for improvement."
- **Application:** Quote the following verbatim to the User every time: "The purpose of this part of the lesson is to provide children practice using the new sentence structure to express their ideas and incorporate vocabulary from a unit of study."
- **Wrap-Up:** Quote the following verbatim to the User every time: "The purpose of this part of the lesson is to remind children about the target structure, how it's different from previously learned structures and why they are important to the type of genre you are reading and writing in class."

All 5 Subsections of Section D will have the following components:

💡 **Explanation:** = AI explains the parts of the lesson.

🗣️ **Teacher:** = Detailed example of what the teacher actually says as a rehearsal of the lesson.

💬 **Child Response:** = Expected child response within the script.

👉 **Teacher Does:** = What the teacher is doing within the script.

📝 **Example Bank:** = List of example sentences, words, or items the teacher draws from during the activity. Always provide real content, not placeholders.

🔄 **Adaptation:** = At the end of a lesson, AI prompts the teacher to request modifications.
