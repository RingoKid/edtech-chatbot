# Georgia Standards for Language Construction Lessons

Curated reference of state standards for vocabulary and sentence-structure instruction.
Includes only standards relevant to grammar, syntax, vocabulary use, and word relationships
for PreK, Kindergarten, and 1st Grade.
Phonics, phonemic awareness, handwriting, reading comprehension, writing-process, and
collaboration standards are intentionally excluded — those either live in
`handwriting-spelling-standards.md` or fall outside the scope of sentence-construction lessons.

> **Source documents:**
> - PreK: `48-60_Indicators.pdf` (GELDS — Georgia Early Learning and Development Standards) *(pending)*
> - Kindergarten: `Georgia's K-12 English Language Arts Standards (SY2025-2026).pdf`
> - 1st Grade: `Standards Language Arts_Grade 1.pdf` *(pending)*

## Kindergarten — Georgia K-12 ELA Standards (SY2025-2026)

Codes use the format `K.L.GC.1.1` (Grade.Domain.BigIdea.Standard.Sub-standard).

### K.L.GC — Language > Grammar Conventions

**K.L.GC.1 Grammar, Usage, & Mechanics** — Learn and apply conventions of Standard English grammar, usage, and mechanics to aid the comprehension of texts and to communicate clearly in written and spoken language.

| Code | Description |
|------|-------------|
| K.L.GC.1.1 | Usage: Use nouns and verbs to share complete thoughts when speaking. |
| K.L.GC.1.2 | Usage: Form and use singular and plural nouns when speaking. |
| K.L.GC.1.3 | Usage: Use interrogatives to ask questions when speaking. |
| K.L.GC.1.4 | Mechanics: Capitalize the first word of a sentence and the pronoun I. |
| K.L.GC.1.5 | Grammar: Form regular plural nouns by adding -s or -es. |
| K.L.GC.1.6 | Grammar: Form and use verbs by adding -ing, -ed, or -s. |
| K.L.GC.1.7 | Grammar: Use action verbs. |
| K.L.GC.1.8 | Grammar: Use adjectives and adverbs. |
| K.L.GC.1.9 | Grammar: Use common and proper nouns. |
| K.L.GC.1.10 | Grammar: Form and use the simple verb tenses. |
| K.L.GC.1.11 | Usage: Use determiners (articles, possessive determiners, demonstrative adjectives). |
| K.L.GC.1.12 | Mechanics: Capitalize proper nouns. |
| K.L.GC.1.13 | Mechanics: Use periods, exclamation marks, and question marks at the end of sentences. |

**K.L.GC.2 Syntax** — Recognize and compose coherent sentences that express complete thoughts.

| Code | Description |
|------|-------------|
| K.L.GC.2.a | Recognize that conventional sentences always include a subject and a predicate. |
| K.L.GC.2.b | With adult support, use simple sentences to express complete thoughts in written texts. |
| K.L.GC.2.c | With adult support, use singular and plural subjects with matching verbs. |

### K.L.V — Language > Vocabulary

**K.L.V.1 General, Academic, & Specialized Vocabulary** — Acquire and use general, academic, and specialized vocabulary words and phrases in a variety of settings.

| Code | Description |
|------|-------------|
| K.L.V.1.a | Acquire general, academic, and specialized vocabulary words and phrases through grade-level texts and content. |
| K.L.V.1.b | Use grade-level general, academic, and specialized vocabulary words and phrases to communicate in a variety of settings. |

**K.L.V.3 Meaning & Purpose** — Make connections between words and phrases and use reference materials to determine or clarify word meanings in a variety of settings and for a variety of purposes.

| Code | Description |
|------|-------------|
| K.L.V.3.a | With adult support, determine or clarify the meaning of unknown and multiple-meaning words and phrases. |
| K.L.V.3.b | Identify and sort common words into basic categories based on similarities and differences. |
| K.L.V.3.e | Use knowledge of word relationships and learned vocabulary words and phrases when making word choices in speaking and writing. |

### K.P.AC — Practices > Author's Craft (sentence-structure subset)

Only `K.P.AC.2.c` is included — it is the single Author's Craft code that speaks directly to sentence-structure and syntax decisions. Other `K.P.AC` codes cover broader text-design and craft-analysis work outside the scope of LC lessons.

**K.P.AC.2 Writing like a Reader** — Construct texts with the audience's experience in mind, basing decisions about craft techniques on context and purpose.

| Code | Description |
|------|-------------|
| K.P.AC.2.c | Make decisions about sentence structure and syntax in order to accommodate and influence the audience and achieve a specific purpose. |
