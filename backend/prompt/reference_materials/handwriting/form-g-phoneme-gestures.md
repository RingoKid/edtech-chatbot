## FORM G: Phoneme Gestures

FORM G
This is FORM G.

A comprehensive list of gestures early education teachers can use to teach phoneme sounds. These gestures pair physical movement with phonemic awareness, which is highly effective for young learners, especially when used alongside handwriting and spelling instruction.

### Consonant Phonemes & Gestures

| Phoneme | Sound | Gesture Idea |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| /b/ | as in *bat* | "Bounce a ball" with both hands |
| /k/ | as in *cat* | Pretend to crack an egg open |
| /d/ | as in *dog* | Tap fingers like playing a drum |
| /f/ | as in *fish* | Wiggle fingers like fish swimming |
| /g/ | as in *goat* | Pull down a pretend goat beard |
| /h/ | as in *hat* | Fan your face (blowing hot air) |
| /j/ | as in *jam* | Pretend to spread jam on bread |
| /l/ | as in *lip* | Move hand in a flowing wave (like licking lips) |
| /m/ | as in *man* | Rub belly like "mmm, yummy" |
| /n/ | as in *net* | Wiggle fingers downward like net catching fish |
| /p/ | as in *pig* | Clap hands softly like popping bubbles |
| /r/ | as in *rat* | Wiggle fingers near mouth like whiskers |
| /s/ | as in *sun* | Trace a spiral sun in the air |
| /t/ | as in *tap* | Tap index finger on the palm of other hand |
| /v/ | as in *van* | Drive an imaginary steering wheel |
| /w/ | as in *wind* | Swoop hands like blowing wind |
| /y/ | as in *yes* | Nod head and give thumbs up |
| /z/ | as in *zoo* | Wiggle index fingers like a buzzing bee |
| /x/ | as in *box* | Cross forearms in an "X" shape |

### Short Vowel Phonemes & Gestures

| Phoneme | Sound | Gesture Idea |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| /a/ | as in *apple* | Pretend to take a bite of an apple |
| /e/ | as in *elephant* | Make an elephant trunk with arm |
| /i/ | as in *insect* | Wiggle fingers like crawling bugs |
| /o/ | as in *octopus* | Wiggle fingers like octopus tentacles |
| /u/ | as in *umbrella* | Pretend to open an umbrella overhead |

### Long Vowel Sounds & Gestures

| Phoneme | Sound | Gesture Idea |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| /ā/ | as in *cake* | Draw a straight line across air (like a cake slice) |
| /ē/ | as in *feet* | Wiggle fingers down toward feet |
| /ī/ | as in *kite* | Trace a kite shape in the air |
| /ō/ | as in *rope* | Pretend to pull a rope |
| /ū/ | as in *cube* | Pretend to blow out a birthday candle (small puff sound) |

### Digraphs & Blends (Consonant Teams)

| Phoneme | Sound | Gesture Idea |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| /sh/ | as in *ship* | Put finger to lips (shhh) |
| /ch/ | as in *chop* | Pretend to chop with side of hand |
| /th/ | as in *thumb* | Show thumbs up |
| /wh/ | as in *whale* | Move hand in a swimming motion |
| /ng/ | as in *sing* | Pretend to sing (open mouth, hand to ear) |
| /qu/ | as in *queen* | Place pretend crown on head |

### R-Controlled Vowels

| Phoneme | Sound | Gesture Idea |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| /ar/ | as in *car* | Pretend to steer a car |
| /or/ | as in *fork* | Pretend to eat with fork |
| /er/ /ir/ /ur/ | as in *her*, *bird*, *fur* | Tap chest like you're thinking "hmm" |

Tips for Implementation:
- Always say the sound aloud as you do the gesture.
- Use gestures during spelling dictation, sound review, and handwriting warm-ups.
- Keep gestures consistent across all phonics lessons.
- Encourage students to mirror the gesture when you say the sound.
