🗣️ **Teacher:**

Sound Puzzle: Children are given two previously learned sounds at a time; they must add in the target sound (either beginning, middle, or end) to create a word. For example, if the target sound is /b/, children are given /a/ /t/; children put the sounds together to make a word (or nonsense word), bat (target sound at beginning) and tab (target sound at end). Children are then given two new sounds along with /b/ to create more words using the target sound. (high-level)

Great! Now we are going to play our Sound Puzzle. We are listening for the /z/ sound at the beginning of the word. The /z/ sound is going to be the beginning sound of the word. Each of you has the /z/ sound in in front of you. I am going to give you two sounds. These are sounds you already know. You are going to put the /z/ sound and the two sounds I give you together to make a word, and it could be a nonsense word. Watch me. I will show you first. Here's two sounds: /a/ and /p/. I am thinking about ordering the sounds to make a word. I am going to place /z/ first, than /a/, then /p/. {teacher sound out the phonemes /z/ /a/ /p/}. When I say go, tell me your word. So, you would say: zap.
My turn. Your two sounds are /i/ and /p/. Put them in order. Get Ready...Go. (children should say zip). {Teacher repeats this process to cement sound to memory then connects to letter form}
