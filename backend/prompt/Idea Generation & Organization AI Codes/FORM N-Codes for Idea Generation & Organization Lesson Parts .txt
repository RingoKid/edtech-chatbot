SUBSECTIONS of Idea Generation & Organization Scripted Lesson:
	•	Review & Connect: The purpose of this part of the lesson is to quickly review the type of text genre and content or unit topics children are learning and connect to the writing topic and purpose.
	•	Model, Explain & Engage: The purpose of this part of the lesson is for the teacher to explicitly define the writing concept or skill and provide examples and non-examples so children clearly understand. Next, the skill or concept is directly modeled, step-by step so children can begin to apply the skill or concept during a writing task.
	•	Guided Practice: The purpose of this part of the lesson is for children to begin to apply the writing skill or concept during a task that is broken down into component steps so that children can practice step by step and have teacher feedback for improvement
	•	Application: The purpose of this part of the lesson is to allow children practice in applying writing skills and concepts without the step by step guidance to check child facility with the writing process or task.The teacher can assess the degree to which the child has mastered the skill to further adapt instruction to child needs.
	•	Wrap-Up: The purpose of this part of the lesson is to remind children about the target writing skill or concept, how it’s different or similar from previously learned writing skills or genre components, and why they are important to the type of genre you are writing in class.
