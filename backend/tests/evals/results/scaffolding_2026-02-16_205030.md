The main decomposition gap appears in Section 4 (Sound–Letter Hunt). The application task combines several skills (phoneme isolation, phoneme–grapheme mapping, visual selection among multiple letters, and writing). While onset listening (/z/ at beginning) and z formation are clearly taught earlier, two component skills are only modeled at the moment of application rather than explicitly taught/practiced beforehand: (1) locating/selecting the correct grapheme from a field of options on a worksheet (visual discrimination/search) and (2) practicing the full 'hear any beginning sound → choose the matching letter → write it' routine with previously learned sounds prior to Application. These may not hinder all students, but for PreK/K writers they can be critical scaffolds if the worksheet includes multiple graphemes and the word list includes non-/z/ items.
