**Judge Summary:** Standards: All listed standards are GELDS (correct system for PreK), all are valid codes from the provided reference, and all are relevant to early phonological awareness, alphabet knowledge, and emergent writing/letter formation.

Developmental fit: The lesson has strong PreK-aligned elements (gesture-based phonemic work, finger writing before pencil, dotted-line tracing). However, it is somewhat ambitious for typical PreK in (1) technical articulation/vocabulary demands, (2) the number of steps/segments in one sitting, (3) expectations for verbal analysis (mouth placement; comparing uppercase/lowercase features), and (4) prior knowledge needed for a multi-letter Sound-Letter Hunt writing task. Consider simplifying oral responses, reducing segments or splitting into two short lessons, and providing more constrained choices/manipulatives (e.g., point to/cover the matching letter, letter tiles/stamps) before expecting independent written encoding.
