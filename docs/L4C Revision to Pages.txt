

Best Practices Modeled Page 

To compose text children need to generate ideas for a specific purpose (idea generation & organization), translate those ideas into language forms (language construction), and transcribe those ideas into printed text (transcription). 

Research shows that when teachers embed direct instruction in idea generation & organization, language construction, and transcription within authentic writing tasks, children make faster gains in literacy, language, and early writing ability. 

Find out the early language and literacy skills to target and best practices associated with helping kids learn those skills. 

Hould be in this order going from left to right 

Idea Generation & Organization    Language Construction    Transcription 

Transcription Page 

To transcribe language into text, children coordinate phoneme–grapheme correspondences with the motor processes required for handwriting and spelling. Instructional targets include letter knowledge, accurate letter formation, phonemic awareness, sound–letter correspondences, directionality, and print conventions.

The following practices help teachers optimize instruction to strengthen children’s recall and written production of sound–letter correspondences, supporting their ability to write words and sentences with increasing accuracy and fluency.
