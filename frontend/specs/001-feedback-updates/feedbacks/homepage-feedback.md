# Homepage

## About Us

Language for Composing (L4C) is an instructional approach grounded in the Science of Literacy, Learning, and Instruction. Through authentic composing, teachers intentionally support young children as they notice, analyze, and use language structures at multiple levels, including sounds, words, sentences, and discourse. L4C helps teachers make language visible, strengthening the connections among language, writing, and learning.

L4C's Advanced Lesson Builder helps teachers translate the science of literacy, learning, and instruction into clear, actionable classroom practice. The builder visually supports lesson sequencing and organization, helping teachers plan instruction in ways that reduce cognitive load while making the language demands of writing explicit. As teachers design composing lessons, they deepen their understanding of how language structures—across phonology, word, sentence, and discourse levels—operate during the writing process and how these structures can be intentionally supported as children generate meaning through text. By making the relationship between language and composing visible, the L4C Lesson Builder supports responsive instruction while strengthening teachers' expertise in language-based writing instruction.

## Principles of the L4C Approach

### Make language visible through responsive and explicit instruction.
Teachers intentionally highlight how language works during composing, helping children notice, analyze, and apply language structures as they write.

### Scaffold children to increase the complexity of their language production and expression.
Instruction supports children in moving from simpler to more complex ideas, sentences, and language forms within meaningful writing tasks.

### Anchor language instruction within authentic composing tasks that build language and concepts.
Language development is inseparable from meaning-making and content knowledge; children learn language best when they use it to communicate ideas that matter.

### Coordinate multiple levels of language to support functional writing.
L4C coordinates spelling and handwriting, sentence construction, and idea development so children experience how language works together to communicate ideas and meaning.

## Educational Enhancement

L4C supports teachers by making language learning visible, connected, and meaningful—both in and beyond the classroom.

### Community Learning Hub
Teachers, families, and researchers come together to share questions, insights, and classroom experiences. This space supports responsive instruction by helping educators learn from one another and from research on how children use language in real writing contexts.

### Knowledge Builder Hub
Short videos for teachers to deepen their understanding of the language skills and processes involved in early writing development.

### Resource Hub
A curated collection of trusted resources to help teachers explore research and practical strategies for supporting language, writing, and learning in the classroom.
